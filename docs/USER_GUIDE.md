# igreen.ai User Guide

This guide explains how to create an account, sign in, and use your impact dashboard to record sustainable actions, build an action plan, schedule routines, and download your records. Start with account creation if you are new, or go straight to signing in if you already have an account.

## Explore the homepage

1. Open the website address provided by your organization or the site owner.
2. Select **Mission** to learn about the site's purpose, **Take action** to explore ways to participate, or **Community** to read about collective action.
3. Select **Join the movement** or **Start your impact journey** to create an account. Select **Sign in** if you already have one.

On a phone or smaller screen, select the menu icon to open the navigation options. Select the igreen.ai logo to return to the homepage. If you are signed in, select **My impact** to return to your dashboard.

## Use the interactive tutorials

1. Select **How to** in the navigation, **How to tutorials** in the footer, or **Explore step-by-step tutorials** on the homepage.
2. Choose a tutorial for creating an account, signing in, building a plan, recording an action, scheduling a routine, uploading Excel records, or exporting your history.
3. Read the instruction and example preview for each step. Select **Next step**, **Previous**, or a numbered step to move through the tutorial.
4. On the last step, select **Mark tutorial complete**. The learning center tracks completed tutorials for this visit; leaving the learning center resets that progress.
5. Use the completion button to create an account, sign in to practice, or open your dashboard if you are already signed in.

Tutorial previews are illustrations and do not update your account. Use the actual dashboard tools to save activities or other changes. Expand the common questions below the tutorials for additional guidance.

The homepage also includes an introduction video. Select **Play introduction** to load the YouTube player, or **Watch on YouTube** to open the video in a new tab.

## Create your account

1. From the homepage, select **Join the movement**.
2. Enter your **Full name**, using at least two characters.
3. Optionally enter your **City or region**.
4. Under **I'm joining as**, select **Personal**, **Business**, or **Corporate**.
5. If you choose Business or Corporate, you may enter your **Organization**. This field is optional.
6. Enter your **Email address**. Use an address you can remember because it is your member login identifier.
7. Create a **Password** with at least eight characters.
8. Select **Create my impact space** and wait for the request to finish.
9. After successful registration, you are signed in automatically and your impact dashboard opens.

If the site says an account with that email already exists, select **Sign in** below the form and use your existing credentials. Select **Back to home** to leave the registration screen.

## Sign in to your member account

1. Open the homepage and select **Sign in**. You can also select **Member sign in** in the footer or **I already have an account** near the bottom of the homepage.
2. Enter the **Email address** you used when registering.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Select **Sign in** and wait while the button shows **One moment…**.
5. Your dashboard opens after a successful sign-in.

The site may open your dashboard automatically when you return with an existing valid session. Use **My impact** if you navigate back to the homepage while signed in.

If you are new, select **Create an account** below the sign-in form. **Admin access** is a separate login for authorized administrators; members should use the email-based sign-in form.

## Read your dashboard

The dashboard shows your recorded progress:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Estimated impact | The total estimated kilograms of CO₂e avoided through your logged actions. CO₂e means carbon dioxide equivalent. |
| Actions logged | The number of activities recorded in your account. |
| Your level | Your current milestone and progress toward the next one. |
| Your personal action plan | Recommendations generated from your goal assessment, once completed. |
| Impact by area | Your all-time estimated impact across the five activity categories. |
| Activity schedules | Your recurring routines, shown after you create a schedule. |
| Recent actions | Up to eight recent entries, with dates and estimated impact. Scheduled and uploaded entries have source labels. |

Impact figures use simple category estimates. They are useful for tracking participation and progress, but are not audited carbon accounting. Use **Export CSV** to download all your activity records, including entries beyond the recent list.

## Build your personal action plan

1. Select **Assess my goals** on your dashboard.
2. Choose one answer to the first question.
3. Select **Next question** to continue. Use **Previous** to review an earlier answer.
4. Answer all six questions about your priorities, pace, setting, transport, meals, and barriers.
5. On the last question, select **Create my action plan**.
6. Read your recommendations under **Recommended for your goals**, including suggested effort and frequency.

Select **Retake assessment** in that section when you want to update your answers. Recommendations do not automatically log activities or create schedules; use the steps below to record what you do.

## Log a sustainable action

1. Select **Log an action**. If your account has no entries yet, you can also select **Log my first action**.
2. Choose an **Impact area** from the categories below.
3. In **What did you do?**, enter a description with at least two characters. Replace the suggested text with your own description if needed.
4. Enter the **Amount** using the unit shown in **Measured in**. For manual entries, enter a number from 0.01 to 100,000.
5. Choose the **Date** the activity occurred. The form initially selects today's date.
6. Optionally add a **Note**.
7. Select **Save this action**.
8. Check **Recent actions** and your dashboard totals for the saved entry.

| Impact area | Unit for the amount | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low-carbon travel | km avoided | Enter 5 for a five-kilometer car trip replaced by cycling. |
| Clean energy | kWh saved | Enter 2 for two kilowatt-hours saved. |
| Planet-friendly food | plant-based meals | Enter 1 for one plant-based meal. |
| Circular living | kg diverted | Enter 3 for three kilograms diverted from waste. |
| Water stewardship | liters saved | Enter 10 for ten liters saved. |

Matching entries are stored only once when the account, date, category, activity description, and quantity match. Changing only the note does not create a separate activity.

## Create and manage a recurring schedule

Use schedules for routines you expect to repeat. The site records due activities automatically, so pause a routine when you stop doing it.

1. In **Recurring schedules**, select **New schedule**.
2. Choose the **Impact area** and enter **What will you do?**.
3. Enter the **Amount** in that category's unit, using the table above.
4. Choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly** under **Frequency**.
5. Choose **Starts on**. The form accepts today or a future date.
6. Optionally enter a **Note**, then select **Create schedule**.
7. Review the routine under **Activity schedules**, including its quantity, frequency, status, and next date.

Select the pause icon to **Pause schedule**, the play icon to **Resume schedule**, or the trash icon to **Remove schedule**. Select **Add another** to create another routine. Removing a schedule stops that routine; previously recorded activities remain in your log.

Scheduled entries may appear after processing rather than at a specific time of day. Due activity is also reconciled when you sign in or open your dashboard. Entries that match an existing activity are skipped.

## Upload activities from Excel

1. In **Excel bulk upload**, select **Upload workbook**.
2. Select **Download the sample workbook**.
3. Open the downloaded `.xlsx` file and read its instructions and examples.
4. Enter your own records on the **Activities** worksheet, starting in row 8. Keep the worksheet name and row 7 headers unchanged.
5. Use the columns **Date**, **Category**, **Activity**, **Quantity**, and **Note** in that order. Enter dates as Excel dates or text in `YYYY-MM-DD` format. Note is optional.
6. Use a category value of `transport`, `energy`, `food`, `waste`, or `water`. Enter a positive quantity in the matching unit from the category table, no greater than 100,000.
7. Keep your records within rows 8–507, up to 500 activities. The importer reads only those rows. Save the workbook as `.xlsx`, with a file size of 5 MB or smaller.
8. Return to the upload window and choose your completed workbook under **Choose your completed workbook**.
9. Select **Upload activities**.
10. Review the counts for activities added, duplicates skipped, and invalid rows skipped. Close the window using **×**, then check your dashboard.

For example, a row can contain `2026-10-04`, `transport`, `Cycled instead of driving`, `5`, and an optional note. Enter these values in separate cells.

The importer checks duplicates both within the workbook and against existing records, including manual and scheduled entries. If rows are invalid, review their dates, categories, descriptions, and quantities before uploading the corrected workbook. Previously imported matching records will be skipped.

## Download your activity records

1. Find **Recent actions** on your dashboard.
2. Select **Export CSV**. It is available after you have at least one recorded activity.
3. Open the downloaded file in Excel or another spreadsheet application.
4. Review the date, category, activity, quantity, unit, estimated impact, and note columns.

The export includes all your activity records. Share the downloaded file only with people you want to receive those details.

## Sign out and understand account privacy

1. Select **Sign out** in the navigation. On a smaller screen, open the menu first.
2. The site returns to the homepage.
3. To access your dashboard again, sign in with your registered email and password.

Member profiles and activity are not displayed publicly. Authorized administrators can view registration details and participation summaries in the protected admin console. Passwords are not displayed there. Sign out when you finish on a shared device.

## Resolve common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Email or password is incorrect | Check that you are using your registered email and the correct password, then try again. |
| You forgot your password | The current site has no self-service password reset. Contact the site owner for help. |
| An account with this email already exists | Use **Sign in** instead of creating another account. |
| Registration or sign-in is temporarily unavailable | Try again later. If it continues, report the displayed message to the site owner. |
| An action does not save | Check the required fields, description length, amount, and date. Read any message shown in the form. |
| An entry is treated as a duplicate | Check for an existing entry with the same date, category, activity description, and quantity. |
| A workbook is rejected | Download a fresh template, keep **Activities** and its headers unchanged, and save as `.xlsx` within the size limit. |
| Some uploaded rows are missing | Review invalid and duplicate counts. Keep all records in rows 8–507 and check each required value. |
| Export is unavailable | Record at least one action and make sure you are signed in. |
| A scheduled activity has not appeared | Check that the schedule is active and due, then reload the dashboard. Contact the site owner if it remains missing. |

## Administrator sign in

This section is for authorized administrators who have received credentials from the site owner.

1. Select **Admin access** in the website footer.
2. Enter your administrator **Username** and **Password**.
3. Select **Open admin console**.
4. Review the community totals and **Community members** table, including account type, organization, location, join date, activity count, and estimated impact.
5. Select **Sign out** when finished.

The current console provides an overview of registrations and participation. It does not provide member editing or password reset controls.
